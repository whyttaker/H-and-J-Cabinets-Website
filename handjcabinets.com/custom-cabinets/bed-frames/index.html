The Product

What is a
custom bed frame?

A custom bed frame is built for the specific bedroom it will occupy — at the right scale for the room's proportions, in a material and finish that coordinates with the surrounding cabinetry and millwork, and to a structural standard that a mass-produced bed frame cannot approach.

Custom bed frames can incorporate headboard panels, integrated storage, floating bases, upholstered elements, and the same premium hardwoods used throughout the rest of the home. They are designed as furniture in the finest sense of the word — both beautiful and structurally rigorous.

Design & Construction

What makes a custom bed frame
exceptional?

The primary quality marker of any bed frame is the rigidity and silence of its structure. A frame that creaks under movement, that shifts on its footings, or that allows the mattress platform to flex over time is a structural failure regardless of how it looks. At H & J, we build bed frames with the same mortise-and-tenon joinery and premium hardware that defines our cabinetry work.

Design coordination is equally important. A custom bed frame built to coordinate with the bedroom's cabinetry — the same wood species, the same finish, the same profile language — transforms the bedroom from a collection of furniture into a composed room. That level of visual coherence is only achievable through a single builder.

  • Solid hardwood constructionPrimary structural members built from premium hardwoods with mortise-and-tenon or dowelled joinery.
  • Rigid, silent platformSlat or platform systems engineered for long-term performance without movement or noise.
  • Coordinated finishPaint or stain matched to the room's cabinetry and trim for complete visual integration.
  • Integrated design optionsHeadboard panels, footboard detail, integrated nightstand cabinetry, and upholstered elements available.
The H & J Standard

Bed frames as part
of the complete bedroom

H & J builds bed frames as part of the bedroom's complete millwork program. The frame coordinates with the wardrobe system, the nightstand cabinetry, and any built-in storage — all drawn as a unified composition and built in our shop to the same standard.

We have built beds that incorporate built-in lighting, integrated charging systems, floating platform designs, and statement headboards that anchor the room visually. In every case, the starting point is the same: what does this room need, and how do we build it to a standard that lasts?

"A bed frame built to the standard of the room around it is furniture in the fullest sense — something you would never think to replace."
