The Maguire Family

Cameo Highlands, Corona del Mar — Lisa Maguire

In Their Own Words

Lisa Maguire's Cameo Highlands home sits at the edge of one of the most coveted stretches of coastline in Southern California. She knew early that the interior would need to earn its place alongside views that change everything. She chose H & J Cabinets to help her get it right.

Reclaimed lumber became the defining material choice — sourced for character, fitted by hand, and finished to highlight rather than obscure the grain and history in each board. The result is cabinetry that feels rooted in the landscape rather than installed over it.

Lisa describes the design process as one of genuine collaboration. H & J brought ideas she had not considered, helped her understand how light would move across different surfaces at different hours of the day, and guided material selections that would age with grace alongside the home itself.

Watching sunsets from a kitchen built with this level of care is, as Lisa puts it, exactly what she imagined — and then some. H & J Cabinets turned her vision into a space that feels both deeply personal and impeccably crafted.
