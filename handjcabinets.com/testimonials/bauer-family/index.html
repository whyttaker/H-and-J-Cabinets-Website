The Bauer Family

Newport Beach — Doug & Kathleen Bauer

In Their Own Words

Doug and Kathleen Bauer approached their Newport Beach home with a clear vision: traditional elegance, executed without compromise. They had worked with enough contractors to know what was possible and what was rare. H & J Cabinets fell into the rare category.

The cabinetry throughout the home is defined by refined white finishes, elevated hardware, and the kind of proportional detailing that only comes from craftsmen who understand traditional construction from the inside out. Doug describes opening a cabinet door for the first time after installation and feeling the difference in weight, in resistance, in the way it settled closed.

Kathleen emphasizes the trust that developed through the project. H & J was not simply executing instructions — they were bringing expertise to every decision, flagging things she had not thought of, and ensuring that each room's cabinetry enhanced the architecture rather than conflicting with it.

The result is a home that feels as refined as the Bauers had always imagined. They describe their experience with H & J as one of the best decisions made across the entire build — the kind of outcome that makes every prior conversation about quality seem like an understatement.
